Quick Start

Navigating Trace

Your browsing history has been successfully mapped into a 3D semantic galaxy. Here is how to move around:

  • Left-Click + Drag: Rotate the camera
  • Right-Click + Drag: Move the camera
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out
  • Double-Click Node: Fly to a specific node
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Click: Open the link in a new tab

The Command Center

  • Settings: Tune the galaxy's shape and aesthetics.
  • Data: Manage your active session or upload a new file.
  • Info: Learn more about the parameters and read how this was made.

You can reopen this guide at any time by clicking the Trace logo in the top-right corner.

Data Collection

Google Takeout

Trace requires a local copy of your browsing history. You can securely download this directly from Google using their Takeout tool.

  • 1: Go to Google Takeout
  • 2: Click "Deselect all".
  • 3: Scroll down and select Chrome.
  • 4: Click "All Chrome data included", deselect everything except "History", and click OK.
  • 5: Scroll to the bottom and click "Next Step".
  • 6: Choose your preferred export settings and click "Export".
  • 7: Extract the downloaded .zip file and locate the History.json file. Upload that file to the Trace dashboard.
