Quick Start

Navigating Trace

Your browsing history has been successfully mapped into a 3D semantic galaxy. Here is how to move around:

  • Left-Click + Drag: Rotate the camera
  • Right-Click + Drag: Move the camera
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out
  • Double-Click Node: Fly to a specific node
  • Cmd/Ctrl + Click: Open the link in a new tab

The Command Center

  • Settings: Tune the galaxy's shape and aesthetics.
  • Data: Manage your active session or upload a new file.
  • Info: Learn more about the parameters and read how this was made.

You can reopen this guide at any time by clicking the Trace logo in the top-right corner.
